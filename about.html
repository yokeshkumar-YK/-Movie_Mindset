THE PERSON BEHIND THE REVIEWS

Hi, I'm Yokeshkumar.

Movie Mindset is my personal movie journal — a place where I keep track of the movies I watch and the thoughts they leave behind.

I'm not trying to be a professional film critic. These are simply my personal opinions about the movies I experience.

Every movie gets my own rating from 1 to 10, along with a detailed review explaining what I liked, what I didn't like and how the movie made me feel.

MY MOVIE MINDSET

Every movie leaves something behind.

Some movies entertain. Some movies challenge us. Some stay in our minds long after the credits roll.

Movie Mindset is simply my way of remembering those experiences.
